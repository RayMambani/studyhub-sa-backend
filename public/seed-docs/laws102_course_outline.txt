LAWS102 — Law of Contract
Course Outline & Assessment Plan (Semester 2, 2026)

MODULE OVERVIEW
An introduction to the requirements for a valid contract under South
African law, and the remedies available when a contract is breached.

WEEKLY SCHEDULE
Week 1-2:  Offer and acceptance
Week 3:    Consensus and the "meeting of minds"
Week 4:    Capacity to contract (minors, mental capacity)
Week 5:    Formalities and contracts requiring writing
Week 6:    Void, voidable, and unenforceable contracts
Week 7:    Misrepresentation and duress
Week 8:    Terms of a contract (express vs implied)
Week 9:    Breach of contract
Week 10:   Remedies (specific performance, cancellation, damages)
Week 11:   Discharge of contracts
Week 12:   Revision

ASSESSMENT WEIGHTING
- Case brief assignment:            20%
- Class test (Week 6):              20%
- Moot/tutorial participation:      10%
- Final exam:                       50%

KEY DATES
- Case brief due:      Week 4, Friday 23:59
- Class test:           Week 6, in-lecture

EXAM TECHNIQUE
Contract law problem questions are usually scored using the IRAC method:
Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion. Always state which requirement of
a valid contract is in dispute before applying the relevant case law.
