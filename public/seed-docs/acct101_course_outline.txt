ACCT101 — Financial Accounting Principles
Course Outline & Assessment Plan (Semester 2, 2026)

MODULE OVERVIEW
An introduction to the accounting cycle, financial statement preparation,
and the fundamental principles (accrual basis, matching, materiality)
that underpin financial reporting for a small trading business.

WEEKLY SCHEDULE
Week 1-2:  The accounting equation, double-entry bookkeeping
Week 3:    Journals and the general ledger
Week 4-5:  Trial balance and adjusting entries
Week 6:    The income statement
Week 7:    The balance sheet
Week 8:    Cash flow statement basics
Week 9:    Inventory valuation (FIFO, weighted average)
Week 10:   Depreciation methods
Week 11:   Bank reconciliations
Week 12:   Revision

ASSESSMENT WEIGHTING
- Weekly problem sets:              15%
- Assignment (adjusting entries):   15%
- Mock test (Week 7):               20%
- Final exam:                       50%

KEY DATES
- Assignment due:     Week 5, Friday 23:59
- Mock test:           Week 7, Friday 09:00

RECOMMENDED APPROACH
Practice the full accounting cycle end-to-end at least twice before the
exam: journal entries -> ledger postings -> trial balance -> adjustments
-> financial statements. Most marks are lost on adjusting entries, so
prioritise that section when revising.
