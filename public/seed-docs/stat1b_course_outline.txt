STAT1B — Introductory Statistics
Course Outline & Assessment Plan (Semester 2, 2026)

MODULE OVERVIEW
Covers descriptive statistics, probability theory, common probability
distributions, and an introduction to hypothesis testing.

WEEKLY SCHEDULE
Week 1:    Descriptive statistics (mean, median, variance, std dev)
Week 2-3:  Probability rules, conditional probability
Week 4:    Discrete random variables
Week 5:    The Binomial distribution
Week 6:    The Poisson distribution
Week 7:    Continuous random variables
Week 8-9:  The Normal distribution and z-scores
Week 10:   Sampling distributions and the Central Limit Theorem
Week 11:   Introduction to hypothesis testing
Week 12:   Revision

ASSESSMENT WEIGHTING
- Tutorial assignments (4):         20%
- Test 1 — probability & discrete distributions:  20%
- Test 2 — continuous distributions & testing:    20%
- Final exam:                       40%

KEY DATES
- Test 1:  Week 6, Wednesday 09:00
- Test 2:  Week 10, Wednesday 09:00

CALCULATOR NOTE
A non-programmable scientific calculator is required for all tests and
the exam. Statistical tables will be provided — you do not need to
memorise distribution tables, but you do need to know how to read them.
